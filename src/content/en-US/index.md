# Bicycle Kitchen (Los Angeles)

![two people working on a bike](/img/wrenching.jpg)

## About

The Bicycle Kitchen/La Bicicocina is a volunteer-run DIY bike repair co-op in Los Angeles.

Our mission is to:

- promote the bicycle as a fun, safe, and accessible form of transportation,
- foster healthy urban communities, and
- provide a welcoming space to learn about building, maintaining, and riding bicycles.

* * *


* * *

## Repairs + Bikes

The Bicycle Kitchen is a learning space! We teach clients to repair bikes. We offer needed tools and used parts.

### Fix Your Bike

Bring your bike in and we can help you to diagnose and fix any issues!

### Get A Bike

We have lots of used bikes to choose from. Our available bikes require some fixing up before riding. We guide clients with these repairs.

### Pricing

Suggested: $8/hr of shop use. Typically $50-400 for whole bikes. Parts priced individually.

Our pricing is sliding scale! We offer a work exchange if you can’t afford a monetary donation.

## Volunteer

Come volunteer! No experience or knowledge needed whatsoever. Stop by during our open hours or email us at [volunteer@bicyclekitchen.com](volunteer@bicyclekitchen.com) to get started.
